Animals and Their Types

Animals are broadly classified into two main groups—vertebrates (such as mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, and fish) and invertebrates (such as insects, mollusks, crustaceans, and arachnids)—with six major classes commonly used in biology. Below is a list of animals and their types to help you get the complete concept about them.
  • Vertebrates(animals with back bone):-
    • Mammals
    • Birds
    • Reptiles
    • Amphibians
    • Fish

  • Invertebrates(animals without back bone):-
    • Insects
    • Mollusks
    • Crustaceans
    • Arachnids
